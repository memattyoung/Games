HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Move with your mouse, or the arrow keys (or A and D). Space launches the ball and P pauses. Drag anywhere on the board and the paddle follows your finger. Tap to launch the ball. If you just sit there, the ball launches itself after seconds. Rude, but fair.

CLASSIC MODE

Plain brick breaker. Break every brick to clear the level, and don't drop the ball. You get lives. No capsules, no creeping wall, no nonsense.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

THE BASICS

Break every brick to clear the level. Every level adds more bricks, and some take a few hits. You get lives. Drop the ball one time too many and it's over.

THE WALL

The whole wall creeps down a row every so often, and faster when there are more bricks left. The WALL bar shows when it's moving next.

If it reaches the red line above your paddle, you lose. Even with lives left.

POINTS

points a brick. Every brick you break before the ball comes back to your paddle grows your combo: ×2, ×3, ×4… Clearing a level gets you a bonus.

Now and then a brick turns rainbow for seconds. Break it for points (times your combo).

MYSTERY CAPSULES

Broken bricks sometimes drop a capsule. They all look the same. Catch one to find out if it loves you or hates you.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Wide paddle for seconds
  • Multiball: two extra balls
  • Slow-mo ball for seconds
  • Sticky paddle for seconds: catch the ball, then launch it
  • Safety net: saves one ball from falling
  • An extra life (rare)

BAD (one of these)

  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk paddle: it lags s behind you for seconds
  • Tiny paddle for seconds
  • Fast ball for seconds
  • Fog for seconds: you can only see near the ball
  • Invisible ball for seconds
  • A steel brick that never breaks

GOOD TO KNOW

Your paddle turns purple when drunk and orange when reversed, and flashes when an effect is about to wear off. You don't have to catch capsules. Sometimes the smart move is to let it fall.

Want plain brick breaker? Pick Classic on the start screen.
