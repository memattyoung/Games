HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Move your paddle with the mouse, or the arrow keys (or A and D). P pauses. Drag anywhere and your paddle follows your finger. There's room under the court for your thumb. You're at the bottom. The smug one at the top is Kevin.

THE MATCH

First to points wins. Get the ball past Kevin's paddle to score, and don't let it past yours. Where the ball hits your paddle decides where it goes, so use the ends to send it flying into the corners.

The longer a rally goes, the faster the ball gets. Beat Kevin and you go straight into the next match, where he's faster and sharper. Your best score is your longest win streak.

Kevin talks trash. He is a computer. Do not take it personally.

CLASSIC MODE

Just you, Kevin and one ball. No orbs, no nonsense. Kevin still has opinions, though.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

MYSTERY ORBS

Orbs keep popping up in the middle of the court. They all look the same. When the ball hits one, whoever touched the ball last gets what's inside. Yes, Kevin too.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Giant paddle for seconds
  • Multiball: two extra balls, for everyone
  • Curveballs for seconds: your shots bend
  • Slow-mo for seconds: the ball slows down when it's coming at you
  • A shield behind your goal that saves one ball

BAD (one of these)

  • Tiny paddle for seconds
  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk paddle: it lags s behind for seconds
  • Fog for seconds: you only see near the ball
  • Invisible ball for seconds
  • The court tilts for seconds and the ball slides sideways

GOOD TO KNOW

Your paddle turns purple when drunk and orange when reversed, and flashes when an effect is about to wear off. Kevin's paddle does the same, and his effects are listed under his paddle so you can laugh at him.

Want a fair fight? Pick Classic on the start screen.
