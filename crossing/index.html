HOW TO PLAY

THE JOB

This is Pip. Pip is small, anxious and lives on the other side of a busy road and a river. Get Pip into each of the empty homes at the top to clear the level. Every level, the traffic gets worse. Obviously.

CONTROLS

Hop with the arrow keys (or WASD). P pauses. Swipe to hop, tap to hop forward, or use the arrow buttons.

WHAT GETS PIP

  • Anything with wheels
  • Water. Pip can't swim. Ride the logs and duck floats instead.
  • Floating off the edge of the screen
  • Hopping into the hedge, or into a home that's already full
  • Running out of time (you get seconds a crossing)

You get lives. Pip is counting on you. No pressure.

POINTS

points for every new row, for getting home plus for every second you had left, and a bonus for clearing the level.

CLASSIC MODE

Just roads, rivers and a very nervous blob. No mystery boxes, no surprises. Pip still can't swim.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

MYSTERY BOXES

Boxes turn up on the road and the grassy strip. They all look the same. Grab one to find out if it's a gift or a prank.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Shield: one free splat, and you're put back on the last safe strip
  • Slow traffic for seconds
  • Time freeze for seconds: everything stops except you
  • Super hop for seconds: two rows at a time
  • An extra life (rare)

BAD (one of these)

  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk hops for seconds: every hop happens s late
  • Fog for seconds
  • Rush hour: the traffic speeds up
  • Swerving cars: they jump between lanes
  • Sinking logs: everything that floats takes little dips
  • A wind gust that blows Pip sideways

AND ALSO

Now and then a road crew closes a lane with cones you can't hop through, and a speed demon blasts down a lane. You'll get a warning. One.

Pip turns purple when drunk and orange when reversed, and flashes when an effect is about to wear off.

Want the plain version? Pick Classic on the start screen.
