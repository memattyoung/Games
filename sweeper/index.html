HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Left click digs. Right click (or Shift+click) plants a flag. F flips the Dig/Flag switch and P pauses. Tap to dig, hold to plant a flag, or flip the Dig/Flag switch under the board. Dig a number that already has enough flags around it and its other neighbours get dug for you.

THE BASICS

Each number says how many bombs touch that square, diagonals included. Dig every square that isn't a bomb to win. Dig a bomb and, well, you'll find out. Your first dig is always safe, because I'm not a monster.

CLASSIC MODE

Just bombs, numbers and a clock counting up. Nothing moves, nothing lies. Suspiciously calm.

1 point per square you dig (×2 on Normal, ×3 on Hard). Win for a bonus, plus points for every second you finish under par ( Easy, Normal, Hard).

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

THE CLOCK

You get on Easy, on Normal and on Hard. It starts on your first dig. Run out and the bombs win by forfeit.

Under seconds the board starts to panic. So will you.

POINTS

1 point per square you dig (×2 on Normal, ×3 on Hard). Win for a -point bonus (times the same), plus points for every second left on the clock.

MYSTERY CAPSULES

Some safe squares hide a capsule. They all look the same. Dig one up to find out whose side it's on.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Free flag: I flag a bomb for you
  • Free dig: a patch of safe squares gets dug for you
  • Time freeze for seconds
  • X-ray: see the bombs nearby for seconds. Blink and you'll miss it.

BAD (one of these)

  • Shuffle: up to bombs you haven't flagged move somewhere new
  • Fog: the numbers vanish for seconds
  • Liar: some numbers are off by one for seconds. They glitch, so you know.
  • Turbo clock: time runs double speed for seconds
  • An extra bomb gets planted somewhere

GOOD TO KNOW

Bombs never move into or out of a square you've flagged, so correct flags stay correct. When bombs move, any numbers that changed flash yellow, and a number that drops to 0 digs its neighbours for free. Digging while lies are flying is allowed. Brave, but allowed.

Want plain old bomb finding? Pick Classic on the start screen.
