# Rage Quit Menu

Matt Young presents games that are fun right up until they aren't.

**[Open the Rage Quit Menu](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/)**

| Game | Play it | About |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Chaos Snake** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/snake/) | Snake where every apple is a mystery. Works on phones and computers. [More](snake/README.md) |
| **Chaos Breaker** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/bricks/) | A brick breaker where every capsule is a mystery, and most of them hate you. [More](bricks/README.md) |
| **Chaos Wings** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/wings/) | Tap to flap. Everything else is out to get you: gravity flips, drunk wings, fog, and pillars that refuse to hold still. [More](wings/README.md) |
| **Chaos Paddle** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/paddle/) | A paddle duel against Tuna Marie, a computer with a big mouth. Mystery orbs shrink paddles, tilt the court and get everyone drunk. Tuna Marie included. [More](paddle/README.md) |
| **Chaos Swarm** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/swarm/) | An alien-wave shooter where every capsule is a mystery and the swarm has opinions about you. [More](swarm/README.md) |
| **Chaos Crossing** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/crossing/) | Pip just wants to get home. Traffic, rivers and a box full of bad ideas have other plans. [More](crossing/README.md) |
| **Chaos Sweeper** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/sweeper/) | Find the bombs before the clock does. The numbers lie, the bombs move, and the first dig is the only free one you get. [More](sweeper/README.md) |
| **Chaos Rocks** | [Play in your browser](https://memattyoung.github.io/Games/rocks/) | Shoot the space rocks while every capsule rolls the dice. Black holes, drunk steering and a UFO with opinions. [More](rocks/README.md) |
