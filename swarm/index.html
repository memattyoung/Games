HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Move with your mouse or the arrow keys (or A and D). Hold Space or the mouse button to shoot. P pauses. Drag anywhere to move. Your ship shoots by itself, because we both know you'd forget.

CLASSIC MODE

Plain old wave shooter. The swarm marches back and forth and creeps down. Shoot them all before they land, and dodge what they throw at you. Some of them get brave and dive at you. You get lives. No capsules, no boss, no combos, no nonsense.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

THE BASICS

The swarm marches side to side and drops a row every time it hits an edge. The fewer of them there are, the faster they go. Clear the whole swarm and a meaner one shows up. You get lives.

If the swarm reaches the red line, it has landed and you lose. Even with lives left. The SWARM bar shows how close they are.

DIVE BOMBERS

Every so often one of them gets brave, breaks formation and dives straight at you. Shoot it for double points, or dodge it and watch it fly home embarrassed.

POINTS

Bottom row 10, middle 20, top row 30. Every kills in a row without missing grows your multiplier: ×2, ×3… up to ×. Miss once and it's gone. Clearing a wave gets you a bonus.

Now and then a rainbow boss floats across the top wearing sunglasses. Hit it times for points (times your multiplier).

MYSTERY CAPSULES

Dead aliens sometimes drop a capsule. They all look the same. Fly into one to find out if it's a gift or a prank.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Spread shot for seconds
  • Rapid fire for seconds
  • Piercing laser for seconds: shots go through them
  • Slow swarm for seconds
  • Shield: takes one hit for you
  • An extra life (rare)

BAD (one of these)

  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk ship: it lags s behind you for seconds
  • Jammed gun for seconds
  • Fog for seconds: you can only see near your ship
  • The swarm speeds up for seconds
  • Boomerang bullets for seconds: misses come back down at you
  • The swarm learns to dodge for seconds

GOOD TO KNOW

Your ship turns purple when drunk and orange when reversed, and flashes when an effect is about to wear off. You blink for a couple of seconds after getting hit, and nothing can touch you while you do. You don't have to catch capsules. Sometimes the smart move is to let it fall.

Want plain old aliens? Pick Classic on the start screen.
