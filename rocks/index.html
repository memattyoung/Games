HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Turn with the left and right arrows (or A and D), thrust with up (or W), hold Space to shoot. Down, S or Shift jumps to hyperspace. P pauses. Push the joystick the way you want to face. Push it all the way to fly that way. Hold FIRE to shoot. WARP jumps to hyperspace.

Everything wraps around: fly off one edge and you come back on the other. So do the rocks. Sorry.

CLASSIC MODE

Plain space-rock shooter. Big rocks split into medium ones, medium into small, small into nothing. Clear the wave and a bigger one shows up. A UFO drops by now and then to shoot at you. You get lives and an extra one every points.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

THE BASICS

Big rocks split into medium ones, medium into small, small into space dust. Clear the wave and a bigger one shows up. A rude UFO drops by now and then to shoot at you. You get lives and an extra one every points.

Hyperspace is a gamble. Now and then you come out the other side as confetti.

POINTS

Big rocks 20, medium 50, small 100, UFO . Now and then a rock turns rainbow for seconds. Smash it for points and a free capsule.

MYSTERY CAPSULES

Smashed rocks sometimes drop a capsule. They all look the same. Fly into one to find out if it loves you or hates you.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Shield: one free crash, for up to seconds
  • Rapid fire for seconds
  • Spread shot for seconds
  • Slow rocks for seconds
  • Smart bomb: wipes out every rock near you
  • An extra life (rare)

BAD (one of these)

  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk steering: your ship reacts s late
  • Fog for seconds: you can only see near your ship
  • A black hole for seconds. It pulls you in, and shields don't help
  • Rocks split into more pieces
  • Slippery space: no brakes at all
  • Curvy bullets that won't fly straight

GOOD TO KNOW

Your ship turns purple when drunk and orange when reversed, and flashes when an effect is about to wear off. You don't have to grab capsules. Sometimes the smart move is to let it float away.

Want the plain version? Pick Classic on the start screen.
