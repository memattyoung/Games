HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Steer with the arrow keys (or WASD). P pauses. Swipe on the board or use the arrow buttons to steer. Hit a wall, a rock or your own body and the game is over. Hit a wall or your own body and the game is over.

CLASSIC MODE

Plain old snake. There's one apple at a time: eat it to grow 1 segment and score 1 point. Each apple makes you % faster.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

HUNGER

Your snake is always hungry and keeps shrinking, and the longer it is, the faster it shrinks. When the hunger bar fills up, you lose a segment.

If your snake shrinks down to nothing, it starves to death.

POINTS

Every apple is worth 1 point. Eat the next one within seconds for a combo: ×2, ×3, up to ×.

Now and then a sparkly rainbow apple appears for seconds. It's worth points.

APPLES

There are always apples and they all look the same. Every apple grows your snake by 1 segment unless it says otherwise. You only find out what you ate afterwards, next to your score.

NORMAL

Grow 1 segment and speed up 5%.

BENEFIT

One of these at random:

  • Grow 1 segment and slow down 10%
  • Grow 2 segments
  • Shield: your next crash is forgiven
  • Ghost: go through walls for seconds

BAD

One of these at random:

  • Speed up 20%
  • Lose segments (never below )
  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk for seconds: turns happen s late
  • A rock appears where you ate it
  • Fog for seconds: you can only see near your head
  • The apples run away from you for seconds

GOOD TO KNOW

Your snake turns purple when drunk, orange when reversed, and see-through as a ghost. It flashes when an effect is about to wear off. Speed always stays between x and x.

Want plain snake? Pick Classic on the start screen.
