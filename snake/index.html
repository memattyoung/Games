HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Steer with the arrow keys (or WASD). P pauses. Swipe on the board or use the arrow buttons to steer. Hit a wall or your own body and the game is over.

HUNGER

Your snake is always hungry and keeps shrinking, and the longer it is, the faster it shrinks. When the hunger bar fills up, you lose a segment.

If your snake shrinks down to nothing, it starves to death.

FOOD

There are always apples on the board and they all look the same. You only find out what you ate afterwards, next to your score. Every apple is worth 1 point.

NORMAL

Grow 1 segment and speed up 5%.

BENEFIT

One of these at random: slow down 10%, or grow 2 segments.

BAD

One of these at random:

  • Speed up 20%
  • Lose segments (never below )
  • Reversed controls for seconds
  • Drunk for seconds: every turn happens s late

GOOD TO KNOW

Reversed and drunk show a countdown above the board. Speed always stays between x and x.
