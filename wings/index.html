HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Space, the up arrow or a click makes Grump flap. P pauses. Tap anywhere to make Grump flap. That's it. That's the whole game. Fly through the gaps in the pillars. Touch a pillar, the ceiling or the floor and it's over. No lives. No second chances. Welcome.

CLASSIC MODE

Just you, gravity and some pillars. 1 point a gap. The pillars speed up and the gaps get tighter the better you do, because of course they do.

Want chaos? Pick Chaos on the start screen.

THE BASICS

1 point for every gap you get through. The pillars speed up and the gaps get tighter the better you do. Grump does not get better. Neither, statistically, do you.

MYSTERY ORBS

Some gaps have an orb floating in them. They all look the same. Fly through one to find out if it's on your side.

GOOD (one of these)

  • Shield: survive one crash
  • Slow-mo for seconds
  • Tiny Grump for seconds: easier to squeeze through
  • Double points for seconds

BAD (one of these)

  • Gravity flip: Grump falls up and a tap pushes it down
  • Reversed: Grump flaps by itself, and you hold to drop
  • Drunk: your flaps arrive s late
  • Fog for seconds: you only see around Grump
  • Wind: gusts shove Grump up and down
  • Heavy: gravity pulls a lot harder
  • Sliding pillars: the gaps move up and down

Bad effects last seconds (fog, ).

OUT OF NOWHERE

Every so often something just happens: the screen flips upside down, an earthquake, a disco, or a big gust of wind. You get one second of warning. You're welcome.

GOOD TO KNOW

Grump turns purple when drunk and orange when reversed, and flashes when an effect is about to wear off. You don't have to fly through the orbs. Grump would advise against it, honestly.

Want plain flying? Pick Classic on the start screen.
